4 By hand

TripsRepsTripsReps
The trip: start on the 50. Ten thrusters, then murder bunny out to a depth, do ten, cross to the opposite side, do ten more, and come back to the 50 for ten more thrusters. The depths get further out each time — 40, 30, 20, 10, goal line. Five thruster sets + five mirrored pairs = 15 sets × 10 reps = 150.
Scoring: reps only, and every exercise is 10. The murder bunnies, reverse murder bunnies and block transports score nothing — they're just how you get there. Reps don't escalate; every trip is the same 150.
Modifications: lunges with block for murder bunny; goblet or air squats for thrusters; chest press to knees for pullovers; hand release merkins for manmakers; curls and OH press for the curl-press-OH-squat; weighted big boy sit-ups for BDE sit-ups. Flag any mods on the submission.
Deadline: midnight the Sunday after release. Best score counts, so you can run it again.
